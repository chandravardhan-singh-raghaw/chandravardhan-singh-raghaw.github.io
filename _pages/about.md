I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the AI Centre of Excellence in Healthcare (TANUH), [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/), Bengaluru, where I work with Prof. Phaneendra K. Yalavarthy on Indian brain foundation models (IndiNeuroFM) and multimodal large language models for clinical report generation.

My research focuses on **multimodal AI for medical imaging**, with an emphasis on vision-language modeling and radiology report generation. I develop contrastive and knowledge-guided methods that make clinical decision-support systems reliable, interpretable and scalable.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Indore (2022–2026) under the supervision of Dr. Nagendra Kumar. Before that I taught computer science for several years as an Assistant Professor and worked in industry as a developer and IT project manager.

**Keywords:** vision-language modeling · medical imaging · multimodal learning · report generation · contrastive learning · foundation models · explainable AI

Research interests
======
* **Radiology report generation** — vision-language models that write clinically grounded reports for chest X-ray, brain and abdominal CT and ultrasound (CliQ-RRG, RDV, UTTAR, SAGE-CT).
* **Cross-modal alignment with clinical knowledge** — contrastive and knowledge-augmented alignment, disease-aware retrieval and in-context learning.
* **Segmentation and classification** — transformer–convolutional hybrids, multiscale attention and state-space (Mamba) models for liver/tumor, polyps, pneumonia, leukemia, retinal and thyroid disease, with semi- and self-supervision when labels are scarce.
* **Foundation models and trustworthy evaluation** — IndiNeuroFM, multi-task chest X-ray and abdominal CT datasets, and explainable-AI benchmarks.
