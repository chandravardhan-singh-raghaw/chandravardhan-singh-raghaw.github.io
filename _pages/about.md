<p class="lead-role">Postdoctoral Researcher &middot; Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru</p>
<p class="lead-badges"><span>Multimodal AI</span><span>Medical Imaging</span><span>Vision-Language Models</span><span>Clinical NLP</span></p>

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [AI Centre of Excellence in Healthcare (TANUH)](https://www.tanuh.ai/), Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, working with [Prof. Phaneendra K. Yalavarthy](https://cds.iisc.ac.in/faculty/yalavarthy/).

My research focuses on developing multimodal artificial intelligence systems for medical image understanding, clinical report generation, and healthcare foundation models. I am particularly interested in vision-language learning, clinical knowledge integration, retrieval-augmented generation, and trustworthy AI for medical applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, where I worked under the supervision of [Dr. Nagendra Kumar](https://sites.google.com/view/nagendrak/). My dissertation, *"Advancing Computer-Aided Medical Diagnosis: Synthesizing Global Context and Local Precision for Pathological Image Analysis of Diverse Imaging Modalities"*, was defended in August 2026.

Research themes
======

My work sits at one intersection — **multimodal AI for healthcare** — and the themes below are facets of it: learning to align medical images with clinical language, using that alignment to write and verify reports, building the foundation models that make this possible, and keeping every step clinically trustworthy.

<div class="themes">
  <div class="theme">
    <h3><span class="theme__n">1</span> Medical Vision-Language Models</h3>
    <p>Aligning medical images with clinical narratives through multimodal representation learning.</p>
    <ul><li>Vision-language pretraining</li><li>Cross-modal contrastive learning</li><li>Disease-aware alignment</li><li>Clinical knowledge integration</li><li>Multimodal large language models</li></ul>
  </div>
  <div class="theme">
    <h3><span class="theme__n">2</span> Automated Medical Report Generation</h3>
    <p>Clinically reliable report-generation systems across imaging modalities.</p>
    <ul><li>Chest X-ray report generation</li><li>Brain CT report generation</li><li>Abdominal CT report generation</li><li>Ultrasound report generation</li><li>Report evaluation and hallucination reduction</li></ul>
  </div>
  <div class="theme">
    <h3><span class="theme__n">3</span> Medical Foundation Models</h3>
    <p>Developing and evaluating foundation models for medical image understanding.</p>
    <ul><li>Indian brain foundation models (IndiNeuroFM)</li><li>Self-supervised learning</li><li>Joint embedding predictive architectures</li><li>Multimodal foundation models</li><li>Domain adaptation and generalization</li></ul>
  </div>
  <div class="theme">
    <h3><span class="theme__n">4</span> Medical Image Analysis</h3>
    <p>Efficient architectures for classification, segmentation and disease detection.</p>
    <ul><li>Medical image segmentation</li><li>Disease classification</li><li>Transformer-based architectures</li><li>State-space models (Mamba)</li><li>Explainable AI</li></ul>
  </div>
  <div class="theme theme--wide">
    <h3><span class="theme__n">5</span> Clinical Knowledge and Trustworthy AI</h3>
    <p>Grounding models in clinical knowledge and making their outputs verifiable.</p>
    <ul><li>Retrieval-augmented generation</li><li>Knowledge graphs</li><li>Clinical reasoning</li><li>Explainability</li><li>Clinical consistency and hallucination assessment</li></ul>
  </div>
</div>
